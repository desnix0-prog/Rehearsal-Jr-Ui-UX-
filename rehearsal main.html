Privacy Policy

What We Collect

This is an interactive interface showcase. Sign-in, Google authentication, email delivery, and parent-account services are not connected. Learning progress and journal notes are stored in this browser on this device. The adventure map does not request your location, and speaking practice does not record or upload audio.

How We Use It

Progress and journal entries power the activities in this browser. Profile details are kept in the current page session. This showcase does not send account or contact-form data to a service.

Parental Controls

The parent-account and time-limit screens are visual previews; they are not linked to an account service. A grown-up can clear locally saved progress from Profile → Reset Saved Progress.

Kids’ Safety

This interface showcase is not a production account service and makes no claim of legal or regulatory certification. Before a public release for children, connect and review secure, parent-approved account, privacy, and data-deletion services.

Data Deletion

Resetting saved progress clears the learning data stored by this page in this browser. It does not delete browser history or data held by other services.
